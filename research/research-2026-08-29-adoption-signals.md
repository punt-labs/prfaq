First-hand notes on the informal adoption evidence referenced in `prfaq.tex`
(`faq:customer-evidence`, fifth paragraph). Recorded to convert second-hand
"I heard" into first-hand "I recorded," per the peer review's recommendation.

## Direct PM conversations (2)

The author has personally spoken with product managers at two separate
companies who report using the plugin. As of this writing:

- Neither conversation has been written up as a structured, anonymized
  account yet.
- Neither company or individual is named here, per the same anonymity
  constraint documented in the FAQ.
- Open follow-up: capture, for each conversation, the company's general
  type (industry, size), how the plugin is being used (which commands,
  how often), whether a document was completed and shared with a
  decision-maker, and whether the same person has returned for a second
  idea. These are exactly the signals `faq:validation` names as
  distinguishing adoption from installation.
